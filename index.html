    Data and limits
    TopographicMOLA elevation and slope (Mars Global Surveyor); 463 m in the sector, 15 km worldwide
    ColourViking orbiter colour mosaic, MDIM 2.1 (USGS)
    OpticalCTX mosaic and HiRISE images (Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter)
    ThermalTES thermal inertia (Mars Global Surveyor)
    SpectralTES Dust Cover Index (Mars Global Surveyor)
    MineralOMEGA pyroxene map (Mars Express; Ody et al. 2012)
    GeophysicalSWIM ice: radar, neutron, thermal, landforms (several orbiters)
    GeologicRobbins and Hynek crater catalog
    SitesHiRISE image centres; Golombek et al. 2021 (LPSC 52, abstract 2420) for AP and EM sites

    The Terrain map is drawn on this device from the MOLA height grid, so it has no missing areas and works without a connection. Colour, Elevation and Imagery are pictures from NASA map servers and need a connection the first time an area is viewed.

    What this map can't see: anything smaller than about 460 m (boulders, small craters, cliffs), loose sand or dust that can trap a rover or tire a walker, and weather such as dust storms. Check higher-resolution imagery before travel. No lander or rover has visited this region, so there are no ground-level photos.

    Elevation: USGS MOLA DEM 463 m. Tiles: NASA Mars Trek. Sites: HiRISE, NASA/JPL. Names: USGS Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature.
