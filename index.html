    What this map can't see

    Anything smaller than about 460 m (boulders, small craters, cliffs), loose sand or dust that can trap a rover or tire a walker, and weather such as dust storms. Check higher-resolution imagery before travel.

    Elevation from the USGS MOLA DEM 463 m. Map tiles from NASA Mars Trek (MOLA relief, and the CTX mosaic by the Caltech Murray Lab). Site positions from HiRISE, University of Arizona. Place names from the USGS Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature.
